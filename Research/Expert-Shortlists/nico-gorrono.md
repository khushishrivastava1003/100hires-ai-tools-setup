## Selected Sources

### 1. Build a complete AI SEO loop: creation, distribution, reporting, and revival

- **Type:** YouTube
- **Video:** “AI SEO Systems for Content Creation, Distribution, Reporting, and Revival”
- **Date:** Not available in scraped file
- **URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uXbEXwSL_7M](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uXbEXwSL_7M)
- **Recommendation:** Treat AI SEO content production as a full system, not just blog writing. Use fanout queries for topic selection, write content with citations and experience, repurpose the article into YouTube and short-form assets, use Google Search Console to find improvement opportunities, and refresh content that is already close to ranking.
- **Evidence:** “We go through content creation, distribution, reporting, and revival.” / “You need to back up every single one of your statements.” / “It’s not just about writing more content, but it’s also about refreshing content that already exists.”
- **Why it matters:** This is Nico’s strongest end-to-end SOP source. It connects research, AI-assisted drafting, distribution, measurement, and refresh into one repeatable production loop.
- **Stage:** Topic research, AI-assisted drafting, citation/fact support, content distribution, reporting, content refreshing, scaling content production
- **Confidence:** High
- **Caveat:** The transcript contains promotional references to tools, skills, and his community. Claims such as YouTube citation frequency and AI-search ranking outcomes should be independently verified before being presented as universal facts.

### 2. Adapt content structure for conversational AI search

- **Type:** YouTube
- **Video:** “Google AI Mode and AI Search Content Strategy”
- **Date:** Not available in scraped file
- **URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xbEAH1NzB5c](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xbEAH1NzB5c)
- **Recommendation:** Structure content for longer, conversational, task-based AI search queries. Rewrite headings as complete questions, answer user tasks directly, add follow-up question blocks, include multimodal assets, optimize image metadata, and test whether pages are usable by AI agents.
- **Evidence:** “The average AI mode query is three times longer than traditional search.” / “Rewriting your headings as complete full questions.” / “Add a follow-up block to every page.”
- **Why it matters:** It gives practical page-structure rules for AI-era content briefs and editing. It is especially useful for turning keyword-based content into question-led, task-led, AI-search-friendly content.
- **Stage:** Search intent, AI-search topic research, content briefs, on-page SEO, multimodal content, agent-friendly publishing
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Caveat:** The `llms.txt` and agentic-readiness claims need caution. Google guidance does not support treating `llms.txt` as required for Google AI visibility, so that part should be marked experimental.

### 4. Fix SEO foundations before chasing AI visibility tactics

- **Type:** LinkedIn
- **Opening:** “Everyone is overcomplicating AI SEO.”
- **Date:** 26 May 2026
- **URL:** [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nico-gorrono_everyone-is-overcomplicating-ai-seo-or-activity-7465069837351014401-NldI](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nico-gorrono_everyone-is-overcomplicating-ai-seo-or-activity-7465069837351014401-NldI)
- **Recommendation:** AI visibility depends first on SEO fundamentals: indexed pages, fast site performance, clear service pages, schema, useful content, entity consistency, and aligned profiles. Add AI-specific tactics only after the core SEO base is fixed.
- **Evidence:** “80% of this is still good old-fashioned SEO done correctly.” / “The extra 20% matters. But it only works after the 80% is fixed.”
- **Why it matters:** It prevents the SOP from becoming AI-hack driven. Before producing or scaling content, the site must be discoverable, understandable, and trusted by search systems.
- **Stage:** SEO foundations, technical SEO, site structure, content quality, AI-search readiness
- **Confidence:** High
- **Caveat:** The post includes `llms.txt` in the AI-specific layer. That should be treated as experimental and not as a proven requirement for Google AI search.

### 5. Use planning controls before letting AI agents build or edit important work

- **Type:** LinkedIn
- **Opening:** “If you are using Claude Code for anything important, do not start with ‘just build this.’”
- **Date:** 6 June 2026
- **URL:** [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nico-gorrono_if-you-are-using-claude-code-for-anything-activity-7469146117503614976-hhR7](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nico-gorrono_if-you-are-using-claude-code-for-anything-activity-7469146117503614976-hhR7)
- **Recommendation:** Before asking an AI coding agent to build or edit important projects, use a setup phase: Workflow, Ultrathink, and Plan Mode. Force the system to define the plan before editing files so it does not misunderstand goals, touch too many files, skip edge cases, or create a confident mess.
- **Evidence:** “Most bad AI coding sessions fail at the beginning.” / “The boring setup step is what makes the execution better.”
- **Why it matters:** This gives the playbook a governance pattern for AI-assisted production systems, internal tools, dashboards, automations, and CMS workflows.
- **Stage:** AI prompting, workflow governance, AI-assisted implementation, quality control, scaling content production
- **Confidence:** High
- **Caveat:** Tool-specific to Claude Code terminology, but the general principle applies broadly: plan before execution and review before shipping.

## Rejected Sources

Excluded sources were mostly promotional/tool posts, repeated AI-search ideas, or posts with less direct SOP value than the selected system, conversational structure, dashboard, foundation, and planning-control sources.

## Takeaway

Nicolas Gorroño’s core viewpoint is that AI SEO is not a replacement for SEO fundamentals; it is traditional SEO plus an AI visibility layer. He is more systems-oriented than purely editorial: content should be created from AI-search questions, distributed across formats, measured against live performance data, and refreshed based on Search Console opportunities. He repeatedly argues that AI becomes useful when it has business context, clear workflows, and human oversight. His approach is practical, tool-heavy, and implementation-focused.

## Strongest Recommendation

Build a complete AI SEO production loop: identify fanout queries, create cited and experience-led content, repurpose it into video/short-form assets, measure performance with Search Console and analytics, then refresh near-ranking content instead of only publishing more.

## Weakest / Least Supported

His recommendations around `llms.txt` and agentic-readiness should be treated carefully. They may be useful experiments for some AI systems, but they should not be presented as proven Google AI visibility requirements without additional verification.
