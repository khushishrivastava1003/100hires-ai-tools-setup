## Executive Summary
Nicolas explains a four-part AI SEO system designed to help brands get cited and recommended by AI search engines. His workflow covers content creation, content distribution, reporting, and content revival. The main idea is that ranking in AI search is not only about publishing blog posts. It requires answering hidden AI search questions, repurposing content across platforms, tracking performance, and refreshing older content that already has ranking potential.

## Key Insights

### Insight 1: AI search content should answer fanout queries
Nicolas emphasizes that AI search engines use hidden related questions called fanout queries. These help identify what content should answer.

### Insight 2: Content needs strong citations and experience
He recommends backing up claims with high-quality sources and adding real experience wherever possible.

### Insight 3: One blog post should become multiple content assets
He turns a blog post into YouTube ideas, short-form clips, slide decks, hooks, and thumbnails.

### Insight 4: Reporting should focus on actionable pages
He uses Google Search Console data to identify page-two opportunities that can be improved.

### Insight 5: Content revival matters as much as new content
Nicolas argues that updating existing content can improve Google rankings and increase chances of being cited by AI search engines.
